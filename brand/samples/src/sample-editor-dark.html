Offline sync launch plan

Owner
NNoor · TTheo
Status
In rollout · 25%MobileDesktop
Target
Thursday, October 15

Offline sync lets people keep writing when the connection drops and merges their edits the moment they’re back. We’re rolling it out in three stages so we can watch conflict rates closely before it reaches every workspace.

Edits are stored locally in an append-only log, then replayed against the server with syncQueue.flush() once the socket reconnects. Conflicts resolve per block, never per page.

Rollout checklist

Ship local persistence behind the offline_v2 flagSep 18
Internal dogfood with the whole team for one weekSep 24
Open to 25% of workspaces and watch the conflict dashboardTOct 1
Write the help article and in-app “You’re offline” banner copyROct 8
General availability and changelog postOct 15

Rollback is one switch. Turning off offline_v2 falls back to online-only editing without losing any queued changes. Keep the dashboard open during each stage.
